## Identity (canonical)

The single recorded home for how this project names itself. Every public
surface — the README strapline, the package or plugin manifest description,
the conventions file's opening — renders from these three lines; a surface
that says something else is drift, fixed by re-rendering from here or by
deliberately changing this block.

- **Title:** Gropius
- **Tagline:** Download MLX models on your Mac and serve them to the rest of your network — an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, in a menu-bar app.
- **Pitch:** Gropius turns one Apple Silicon Mac into a shared local-inference server. Browse and download MLX models from HuggingFace, and serve them over an OpenAI-compatible API to every other machine and user account on your network — anything that talks to ChatGPT can talk to your Mac.
